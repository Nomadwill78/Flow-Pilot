# FlowPilot Sales Email Templates

Copy, personalize, and send. These are battle-tested cold outreach templates.

## Template 1: The Problem-First Cold Email

**Subject:** Your users are struggling with [App Name]'s dashboard

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

I was checking out [App Name] and noticed your dashboard has a lot of powerful features — filters, reports, multi-step workflows.

Here's what I bet is happening: a chunk of your users open the app, get overwhelmed, and either open a support ticket or churn.

We built FlowPilot to fix exactly that. It's an AI copilot that embeds into any web app and lets users control the interface through natural language. Instead of hunting through menus, they just type "show me last month's revenue" and it's done.

**The kicker:** It takes under an hour to integrate. No rebuilding. No training data. Just a JavaScript widget and a proxy server.

Worth a 10-minute call to see if it fits?

[Your Name]
FlowPilot

P.S. — We're running a free 2-week pilot for 5 companies this month. Happy to slot [Company] in if you're interested.
